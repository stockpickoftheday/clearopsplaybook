Terms

These terms cover buying and using the SOP packs and downloads on this site.

What you are buying

Each pack is a set of digital files (PDF, Word, and Markdown). You buy the files once. There is no subscription, no monthly fee, and no software to install. Prices are in US dollars and include any sale discount shown on the page.

Licence

When you buy a pack, you get a licence to use it in one business. You may print it, edit it, and share it with your own staff inside that business. You may not resell it, give it away, share it with another business, or post it online for others to download.

The free versions are for trying a pack before you buy. They are covered by the same licence: use them in your own business, do not pass them on.

No refunds

All sales of digital downloads are final. A download cannot be returned once you have the files. Every pack has a free version with one or two complete procedures from that exact pack, so please download the free version first and make sure the pack fits your business before you buy.

If a file you paid for will not download or is damaged, email us at hello@clearopsplaybook.com and we will send you a working copy.

Not legal or professional advice

The packs are practical working documents. They are not legal advice, and they are not a substitute for professional advice. Some packs touch on regulated areas — childcare, privacy law, food safety, medical devices. You are responsible for checking your own local rules and getting professional advice where your business needs it. Following a procedure in a pack does not guarantee you meet any law, standard, or certification.

As-is

The files are provided as-is. We do our best to keep them accurate and up to date, but we do not guarantee they fit every business or every situation. Use your judgement and change the details to fit your business.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version will be posted on this page. Last updated: November 2026.
