Privacy & Data Request Kit

When a customer asks what data you hold about them — or asks you to delete it or fix it — you need an answer fast, and it has to be the right one. This kit gives you ten ready-to-use procedures that walk a small business through every kind of data request, from logging it on day one to answering it, deleting data, handling a breach, and training staff. Every procedure is written out in full using an example business, Fern & Field Coaching, so you can follow it as-is and adapt the names to your own business. No blanks, no legal jargon — just plain steps with deadlines, checks, and what to do when things go wrong.

Sooner or later, a customer will ask what personal data you hold about them. It might be a simple email — "what do you have on me?" — or a formal request for a copy of everything, a correction, or a full deletion. However it arrives, you have to spot it, answer it on time, and prove what you did.

Guessing your way through it risks missing the deadline, sharing data with the wrong person, or deleting something you needed to keep. This kit takes that worry away. It contains ten complete procedures that cover the whole journey of a data request, written in plain English and filled in with realistic detail — no blank templates, no boxes left for you to work out.

Each one follows the same small business throughout: Fern & Field Coaching, a two-person coaching business with a client list, an email list and an online booking system. You read the procedure, swap in your own business details, and follow the steps. You start by taking the request in properly: logging it the same day, starting the deadline clock, and acknowledging it within two working days.

Then you check the person really is who they say they are, so client data never goes to a stranger. Next you find all their data — in the client list, the email list, the booking system, invoices, emails and even paper files — and you answer the access request with a clear pack, sent safely. The kit also covers the harder cases.

Deleting someone's data is walked through place by place, including what you may have to keep for tax reasons and how to confirm the deletion in writing. Fixing wrong data covers every system it appears in. If a request is too broad, repeated, or needs more time, there is a procedure for refusing politely or asking for an extension — including telling the person how to complain.

A breach procedure tells you what to do in the first hour, who to tell, and how to record it. A request log procedure keeps one honest record of everything, and the final procedure trains your staff on privacy basics in a single 30-minute session. Every procedure names who does it, gives numbered steps with real deadlines, includes a quality check by a second person, explains what to do when it goes wrong, and says what records to keep.

A note in the kit reminds buyers that these are general guidance, not legal advice, and that they must check their own local privacy law. If your business holds customer or client data — names, emails, bookings, payments — this kit gives you a calm, repeatable way to handle every request that comes in, and the paper trail to prove you did it right.
