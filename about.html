About ClearOps Playbook

ClearOps Playbook sells ready-to-use standard operating procedures (SOPs) and operations playbooks as digital downloads for small business.

What we sell

Every pack is a set of procedures for one kind of business — a workshop, a restaurant, a daycare, a catering company, and more. The procedures are written out in full, with real steps, real numbers, and real examples. They are not blank templates. You download the pack, change the names and details to fit your business, and start using it.

Each pack comes as PDF, Word, and Markdown files, so you can print it, edit it, or copy it into your own documents.

Try before you buy

Every pack has a free version: one or two complete procedures from that exact pack, in the same PDF, Word, and Markdown formats. Download the free version first and see the quality. If it is not what you need, you have spent nothing.

Downloads only

There is no subscription and no monthly fee. You buy a pack once and it is yours to use in your business. There are no accounts to create and no software to install.

No refunds — and why

Digital downloads cannot be returned once you have the files, so all sales are final. That is why every pack has a free version: you can check the real thing before you pay anything.

What our SOPs are not

Our packs are practical working documents, not legal advice. Some packs touch on regulated areas — childcare, privacy law, food safety, medical devices. The procedures in them are a starting point: you are responsible for checking your own local rules and getting professional advice where your business needs it.

Questions about a pack, a broken download, or a suggestion for a new pack? Email us at hello@clearopsplaybook.com or use the contact page.
