Running a small welding shop means juggling enquiries, quotes, cutting lists, welding, painting, fitting and paperwork — usually with only two or three people and no office manager to keep it all straight. The Welding & Fabrication SOP Pack gives your shop a written way of doing every job, from the first phone call to the final sign-off, so nothing gets missed and everyone works the same way. The pack follows one invented example business, Northgate Welding & Fabrication, a three-person shop making gates, railings and custom steelwork.

Every procedure is written out in full around that shop, with real numbers, real steps and real decisions — not blank templates for you to fill in yourself. You can use the procedures as they are, or change the names, prices and timings to match your own shop. Inside you will find ten procedures that cover the whole life of a job.

It starts with handling a customer enquiry and doing a proper site visit, then pricing the job and writing a clear quote. It covers taking a deposit and ordering steel, the daily safety and hot work check, cutting and fitting up, welding and inspection, grinding and painting, and getting the finished work to site and fitted square and level. It finishes with handover and sign-off, collecting the final payment, and handling changes, defects and rework when things do not go to plan.

Safety is built into the procedures as practical steps, not legal advice: protective equipment, gas bottle checks, fume extraction, hot work checks and the 30-minute fire watch after welding. Each procedure ends with a quality check, what to do if it goes wrong, and what records to keep, so you can prove the work was done right. The pack is written in plain English a small business owner can follow, and it comes in PDF, Word and Markdown, so you can print it, edit it, or keep it on a phone or tablet in the workshop.

If you run a welding shop, a gate and railing business, or you fabricate steelwork on your own, this pack gives you a complete, ready-to-use set of procedures for a three-person shop — and a calmer, more organised way of working.
